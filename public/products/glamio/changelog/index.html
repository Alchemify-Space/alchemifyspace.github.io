Changelog

What's new in Glamio

Every release, newest first. The same list is in CHANGELOG.md in your download.

1.1.0 Latest

  • New design ("Noir Atelier"): a more premium look for the phone app, the web admin and the booking website: ink and champagne on warm bone in light mode, champagne on espresso in dark mode, new fonts, and a new logo, app icon and start screen.
  • Booking website: clients book from a link, with no app to install. Services, stylist or "any", a free time in the salon's time zone, sign in with the emailed code. Under My bookings they change or cancel, add the booking to their calendar, and edit their name and phone.
  • Booking emails: confirmation, changes, cancellations, and reminders 24 h and 1 h before, for clients without the phone app.
  • Moved: the web admin is now at /admin; the plain website address is the booking page. The admin/ folder is now web/.
  • Web admin: a top bar replaces the side menu, a numbered setup checklist on the dashboard, filter services by Active and Hidden, transparent logos stay visible in light mode.
  • Phone app: free times in even columns, an "up next" card for stylists, sign-in during a booking continues straight to the review step, clearer error messages, and the Android status bar blends into the page.
  • Optional: Google sign-in on the booking website.
  • Updating from 1.0: follow documentation → Installing updates → Updating from 1.0 to 1.1.

1.0.1

  • Security: only the database can queue notifications and reminders.
  • Android: SDK versions written as numbers (compile and target SDK 36, minimum SDK 24).
  • iPhone: privacy manifest included.
  • Documentation: a statement on the use of AI tools.

1.0.0

First release.

  • Client app: browse services, book one or more services, choose a stylist or "anyone", see free times, confirm, move and cancel, booking history, profile and account deletion.
  • Stylist app: live schedule, booking details, completed and no-show, walk-in bookings.
  • Web admin: dashboard, day and week calendar, walk-in and phone bookings, services, stylists, working hours, time off, closures, clients, settings, stylist portfolios, app access for staff.
  • Notifications: push for new, moved and cancelled bookings, and reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before a visit.
  • Sign-in: email code on every phone, plus Google sign-in on Android (optional).
  • Languages: English, Spanish, French and Arabic (right to left). Light and dark themes.
  • Quality: double bookings blocked by the database, free times worked out in the salon's time zone, row-level security on every table, automated tests for the app, the admin and the database.
