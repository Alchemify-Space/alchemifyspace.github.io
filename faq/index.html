FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Working with agencies

Will our client know you exist?

Only if you want them to. We sign your NDA, work under your brand and never contact your client directly.

Who owns the code?

You do, once paid. It lives in your repository from day one.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most agencies send one real ticket as a paid trial task, judge the code, then move to a project or retainer.

Can you work on an existing codebase?

Yes. We read it first, follow its conventions and ship changes as reviewed pull requests.

Payments and practicalities

How do payments work?

Milestone payments for projects, monthly for retainers. We invoice as a registered company in Pakistan.

What time zone do you work in?

Pakistan (UTC+5). We keep at least 4 hours of overlap with your working day; US calls by arrangement.

What happens after launch?

A support period is agreed in the quote. After that, maintenance runs on a small monthly retainer or per task.

AI features

Do we need to rebuild our app to add AI?

No. The packages add one feature to the app you already have, with a fixed scope and price.

What does the AI cost to run each month?

It depends on usage. We estimate the monthly provider cost during the audit, before you commit.

What happens to our data?

It stays in your backend. API keys live on the server, and we use provider settings that keep data out of model training where offered.

Glamio and ready-made apps

When can I buy Glamio?

It is planned for CodeCanyon in January 2027. Contact us to be notified or to get early demo access.

Do I need my own server?

No. Glamio runs on Supabase, so there is no PHP server or shared hosting to manage.

Can you set it up and brand it for me?

Yes. Branded setup with store publishing starts from $1,500.
