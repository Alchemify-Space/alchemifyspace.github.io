Bijli Unit Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Bijli Unit Tracker is made by Alchemify Space (ALCHEMIFYSPACE (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED), Sahiwal, Punjab, Pakistan. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your meters, readings and bills stay on your phone.

What the app stores on your phone

When you add a meter or a bill, Bijli Unit Tracker saves it on your device: the meter name you choose, the meter readings you type or scan, the dates, the unit limit, and the figures read from your bill (reference number, meter number, units, amounts and the 12-month history). Your reminder and language settings are saved there too. We do not receive this data. It never goes to our servers, because we do not run any servers for the app.

What we do not store

  • Your name, father's name and address on the bill. The app does not save them.
  • The bill PDF itself and the meter photos. They are read on your phone and then discarded.
  • Your location.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or give an email address or phone number.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with anyone.

Reading your bill

When you choose a bill PDF, or open one from WhatsApp or Files, the app reads the text inside it on your phone and fills in your meter, readings and history. This is done with ordinary text rules, not with an online service. The file is not uploaded anywhere. While it is being read, Android may hold a temporary copy in the private storage of the app, which the app removes afterwards.

Photos of your meter

  • The photo is taken with your phone's own camera app, or you choose one with the system picker.
  • This is a beta feature. The number is read on your phone using Google ML Kit text recognition. The picture and the text are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Like other apps that use ML Kit, the ML Kit library may send small technical reports to Google, such as device model, app version and error or performance information. These reports do not contain your photo, your meter or your bill. See Google's ML Kit terms and data disclosure for details.
  • The app deletes its copy of the photo after reading it. Only the number you confirm is saved.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Bijli Unit Tracker shows reminders that are created on your phone, for example when you are close to your unit limit or when it is time to add a reading. No server is involved. You can turn each reminder off in the app or in Android settings.

Android backup

If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the data of this app in your Google account backup, and restore it when you set up a new phone. This is controlled by your phone and your Google account, not by us, and we never receive it.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or open Android Settings, then Apps, then Bijli Unit Tracker, then Storage, and choose "Clear data". Either way your meters, readings and bills are removed from your phone.

Children

Bijli Unit Tracker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add accounts, ads, analytics or cloud features, we will update this policy before the update is released.
