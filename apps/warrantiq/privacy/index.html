Privacy policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ALCHEMIFYSPACE (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED, trading as Alchemify Space ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the WarrantIQ mobile application, our website at alchemifyspace.com/apps/warrantiq, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service. Our Terms of use also apply.

1. Who we are

WarrantIQ is a warranty-tracking product operated by ALCHEMIFYSPACE (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED, a company registered in Pakistan (SECP no. 0357394), with its registered office at Chak No. 60-61 GD, Tehsil and District Sahiwal, Sahiwal 57000, Punjab, Pakistan. We are the "controller" of your personal data. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at support@alchemifyspace.com.

2. Information we collect

Account information. When you register, we collect your email address, your name if you give it, and your sign-in credentials. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not stored in plain text by us. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive the basic profile details Google shares with us (your name and email address).

Warranty and asset data. Information you enter or generate in the app, such as product names, brands, categories, purchase dates, warranty end dates, serial numbers, and notes.

Photos and documents. Images and PDFs you capture, choose, or share into WarrantIQ from another app (for example a receipt sent from WhatsApp, an appliance label, or a warranty card). They are stored in your private receipt vault, and photos can be used for optional AI-assisted field extraction.

Subscription and purchase data. If you subscribe to WarrantIQ Premium, payment is processed by Google Play. We receive subscription status and entitlement information through our billing partner (RevenueCat) so we can unlock Premium features. We do not receive or store your full payment card number.

Device and app information. To send notifications and keep the app working, we store a push notification token, a device identifier, your device model and operating system, the app version, and the date you last used the app.

Usage and diagnostics. We collect product analytics (such as which features are used) through Mixpanel and, in production builds, crash and error reports through Sentry, to improve reliability and performance.

Device permissions. With your permission, the app may use your camera (to scan receipts, labels and barcodes), your photos or files (to attach documents), and notifications (to remind you before warranties expire). You can turn each one off in your device settings.

Website. This marketing site is static and does not use advertising cookies or account login. Our hosting provider may process standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and delivery.

3. What stays on your device

Some features work on your phone and do not send data to us:

  • Reminders. Expiry reminders (30, 7 and 1 day before cover ends) are scheduled on your phone from the dates you saved.
  • Barcode scanning. Barcodes are read on your device. The camera picture is not sent to us.
  • Home-screen widget. The widget shows the name and expiry date of your next warranty on your home screen. Anyone who can see your phone screen can see it. You can remove the widget at any time.
  • Claim messages and support contacts. When you make a claim, the app writes a message from your warranty details and opens WhatsApp, your email app, or your phone app for you to send it. A support contact you type for a brand is saved only on your phone.
  • Proof-of-purchase PDFs and backup files. These files are created on your device. You decide whether to share or save them, and where. We do not receive them. A backup file contains your warranty details but not your receipts and documents.

4. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Store your assets, documents, and reminders securely
  • Extract warranty-related fields from images when you use AI scan features
  • Show reminders and send notifications before warranty expiry
  • Process subscriptions and enforce free-plan limits
  • Understand usage patterns and fix bugs
  • Respond to support requests and meet legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Why we may process it (legal bases). Where laws such as the GDPR apply: to run your account, store your data, and provide Premium we rely on performing our contract with you; to keep the Service secure, fix bugs, and understand usage we rely on our legitimate interests; to send notifications and use your camera or files we rely on your permission, which you can withdraw in your device settings; and to keep records or answer legal requests we rely on a legal obligation.

5. AI processing

When you use AI scan, the receipt or label image is sent to a third-party AI provider (through OpenRouter) to suggest the product name, dates, warranty term, and serial number. You can review and edit every field before saving. Results can be wrong, so please check them. Do not scan images that contain information you do not want processed for this purpose.

6. How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Service, under terms that require them to protect your data. These include:

  • Supabase — sign-in, database, and private file storage
  • OpenRouter — AI vision processing for scan features
  • Google Play & RevenueCat — in-app purchases and subscription status
  • Google — optional sign-in with Google
  • Google Firebase — push notifications and remote configuration
  • Mixpanel — product analytics
  • Sentry — crash and error reports

When you send a claim message, a PDF, or a backup file to another app (such as WhatsApp or your email), that app handles it under its own privacy policy. We do not receive it.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or users, or in connection with a merger or sale of the business (with notice where applicable).

7. Data retention

We keep your account data, assets, and uploaded documents while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account, warranties, and uploaded files from our systems, normally within 30 days, and from backups within a further 30 days. We may keep limited information longer only where the law requires it or to resolve disputes and prevent fraud (for example purchase records). Analytics and crash data are kept in a form that is not used to identify you for as long as needed to improve the app. You can delete single warranties or documents at any time in the app.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and row-level security so each person can reach only their own records. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you
  • Export your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Ask for a copy of your data in a portable format

We answer requests within one month (or the time your law sets). If you are in the EU, EEA, or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority. If you live in a US state with a privacy law, you may have the rights above and the right not to be treated unfairly for using them. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

You can edit or delete individual warranties and documents in the app. To delete your whole account, open WarrantIQ, go to Account, and tap "I want to delete my account", or follow the steps on our account deletion page, or email support@alchemifyspace.com from the address linked to your account. We may need to verify your identity before we act on a request.

You can turn notifications off in the app's settings or in your device settings. You can manage a Premium subscription in Google Play subscription settings.

10. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

We and our service providers may process data in countries other than where you live, including Pakistan, the United States, and the European Union. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as the standard contractual clauses or our providers' data-protection commitments for cross-border transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date. Material changes may also be shown in the app or sent by email where appropriate. Continued use after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
